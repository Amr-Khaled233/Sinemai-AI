BRAND FILM BRIEF — 60s hero film, luxury watch launch

Scene 1 — INT. ATELIER - DAY
Macro coverage of a watchmaker's hands setting a movement. Soft north-facing window light, deep shadow, one bounce card. Table-top, locked off, slow dolly in on a slider. No dialogue.

Scene 2 — INT. ATELIER CORRIDOR - DAY
Steadicam follows the watchmaker walking the length of a corridor. Practical sconces plus a soft overhead wash. Shallow depth of field, warm tones.

Scene 3 — EXT. RIYADH SKYLINE ROOFTOP - DUSK
Talent on a rooftop at golden hour, city behind. Long lens, handheld, backlit. We need a large diffusion frame for the close-ups and a reflector for fill as the sun drops.

Scene 4 — INT. STUDIO LIMBO - DAY
Product on a turntable against black. Hard single source with a gridded soft box, controlled flare on the crystal. High frame rate for the reveal.

Scene 5 — EXT. DESERT DUNE - NIGHT
Hero shot: the watch on a plinth, dune ridge behind, night sky. Needs generator power, a strong key at distance, and haze. Aerial establisher of the dune field at blue hour.
